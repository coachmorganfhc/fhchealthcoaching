CoachRx integration

CoachRx handles programming, macros, tracking. This portal handles the relationship layer.

  • Weekly automated check-in — edit per client every week. Never generic. Always tuned to current pillar focus.
  • 2-3 touchpoints minimum — every client, every week. Replies to CoachRx updates. Non-negotiable.
  • Programming & macros — inside CoachRx, in line with assessment & session updates.
  • Cross-coach visibility — for combined health + fitness clients, both coaches review logs.
The 12-week chapter
Every 12 weeks, a deliberate review.

Ongoing coaching needs structure or it drifts. 12-week chapters — each ending with a dedicated review that pulls back from the week-to-week and looks at the arc.

During the 12 weeks
· Biweekly or monthly sessions
· Mon/Wed/Fri communication rhythm
· 2-3 CoachRx touchpoints/week
· Programming evolves inside CoachRx
At the 12-week review
· Visual progress report (data + photos)
· What worked, shifted, surprised
· Recalibrate goals for next 12 weeks
· Updated programming roadmap delivered
